TailorFlowA Unified and Expressive Generative Model for 3D Clothing

Abstract

Realistic and editable modeling of human avatars is crucial for applications like telepresence, entertainment, and virtual try-on. However, existing generative methods typically model clothing and the human body as a single surface, limiting their ability to represent layered clothing realistically. We introduce TailorFlow, a hierarchical latent point flow model for unified and expressive generative modeling of 3D clothing. TailorFlow is the first model capable of representing clothing of all topologies within a single framework. It can generate diverse clothing shapes conditioned on the clothing style, underlying body shape, and fine-grained details like sleeve length and hem length. Thanks to our body-aware encoding, TailorFlow produces clothing that is well-aligned to the body and respects the layering order of body and clothing. We make the latent space coverage-aware: the model is trained to trim and grow garments, so clothing can be edited by painting its occupancy on the body. TailorFlow can be applied to inverse problems, such as fitting layered clothing to scans with occluded garments.

How it works

A body-aware VAE encodes each garment, given as a point cloud, into a global shape code and a latent point cloud on a canonical body. The decoder places the garment on any body and any coverage. A rectified-flow prior samples both latents for a chosen garment class in 64 steps, and the decoded points are meshed with screened Poisson reconstruction.

Interpolate between any two garments

Garments morph smoothly and at an even pace, within a class and across classes: straps appear on strapless dresses, necklines rise, sleeves and hems grow, skirts flare, and a dress can split into a jumpsuit.

Grow and trim by painting coverage

Paint where the garment should cover the body. TailorFlow keeps the cloth away from the edit unchanged and generates only what is new, so sleeves, hems, shoulder straps and necklines change with a clean seam, down to turning a jumpsuit into trousers.

Fits every body shape

The same garment adapts to short, tall, slim and heavy bodies of either gender without passing through the body.

Layered clothing from scans

Given a scan with clothing labels, TailorFlow fits each layer separately, including the parts hidden under outer garments.
