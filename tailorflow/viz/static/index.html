How the edit works

One edit trims and grows. Cloth more than 8 cm from any painted change is kept exactly; the rest is regenerated by the prior under the new mask and blended into the kept cloth, so the join has no gap; new cloth the VAE judges outside the paint is dropped, so one-sided edits stay one-sided. The edit appears as a new card next to the original.

What transfer does

The same latent decoded on random bodies. Pick a card to inspect it; the body panel under the viewport then edits that body and the garment follows.
